⚠ Verify every job before you apply

These buttons open live searches on job sites. Cyber Kalari does not post, check or guarantee these listings, and fake jobs do appear on every site. You are responsible for checking each job yourself.

Before you apply: 5 quick checks
  1. Find the company's official website and LinkedIn page. Look for a real office address and real employees.
  2. See if the same job is on the company's own Careers page. Apply there or through the job site, not through a stranger's WhatsApp.
  3. Offers and interview calls should come from the company's email domain (name@company.com), not Gmail, Yahoo or Outlook.
  4. Read the post date. Jobs older than 30 days are often already filled.
  5. Never share your Aadhaar, bank details or OTPs until you have a written offer and have confirmed the company is real.
Warning signs of a fake job
  • They ask for any payment: registration, training, laptop, visa, medical or "security deposit". Genuine employers don't charge candidates.
  • An offer with no interview, or a salary far above normal for the role.
  • Pressure to decide today, or chat-only contact with no phone call or office.
  • Spelling mistakes, vague job duties, or a company name you can't find online.
Applying for jobs abroad (Gulf and other countries)
  • Get the offer letter and contract in writing (salary, role, visa type) before you travel or pay for anything.
  • If a recruiting agent is involved, check that they are a registered recruiting agent on the Government of India's eMigrate portal (emigrate.gov.in).
  • Never travel on a visit visa for a "job" promise. Work visas are arranged by the employer.
